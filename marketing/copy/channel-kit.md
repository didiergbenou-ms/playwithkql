## Before posting

The destination is intentionally unpublished. The team can publish these
assets to GitHub now; post the play invitation publicly once the game page is
available. Check the page and a printed QR when the team launches, not on
every asset-editing pass.

Use the visible label **Play KQL Quest**. Teams and email can attach the URL
with their Insert link controls. LinkedIn and Instagram do not render Markdown
links like GitHub does: do not paste the raw Markdown into those captions.
Use the image QR and, where supported, a labelled profile link or Story sticker.
QR codes do not hide their destination from a scanner.

For LinkedIn and Teams, start with the 30s landscape video. Instagram has
vertical cuts and a matching cover. The carousel order is feed cover, query
slide, gameplay slide. Leave space between posts to respond to real feedback.

The README is the source for current content counts. Do not describe these
older captures as a tour of every current question or difficulty tier. AI
coaching, adaptive missions and formal skill measurement remain plans, not
features demonstrated by these assets. Do not add invented completion times,
testimonials or learning gains.

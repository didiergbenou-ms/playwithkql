## Timing and delivery

The six delivery windows start at 0.5, 7, 19, 28, 39 and 49.5 seconds. They end
at 3.7, 11, 23.5, 33.5, 43.5 and 53.5 seconds. The SRTs use actual speech end
times; silence fills the rest of each window. There is no narrator over the
final title and QR card at 54-58 seconds.

Use a clear, conversational read. The main take is restrained; the deadpan take
is drier; the learner take has warmer phrasing. Do not force a low pitch or
stretch a short sentence to fill its slot. For later recordings, pronounce KQL
as "kay cue ell" and Kusto as "KUSS-toh". The game name and visible captions
are not read back by the narrator.

Captions are supplied separately, not burned over the existing terminal and
editorial text. Check the mix by ear on headphones and laptop speakers before
external use. Timing, sample preservation and non-clipping checks do not
substitute for listening approval.
